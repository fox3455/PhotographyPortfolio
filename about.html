About

Hi, I'm Caramon "Fox" Stanley.

I got into event and esports photography because I love capturing the energy and emotion of competitive gaming.

Background

I started shooting esports events in 2023 for a top 8 team called TSS at HCS Charlotte and have since covered other major events like the Halo Championship Series World Championship, Comic-Con Winter and Summer in Stockholm, and DreamHack. My work focuses on capturing the intensity of competition, the excitement of the crowd, the stories behind the players and fans, and the incredible cosplayers showing their craftsmanship and skill.

What I shoot

  • Esports events Halo Championship Series, DreamHack
  • Fan conventions Comic-Con, Närcon
  • Available for: Editorial, event coverage, team & sponsor work
