About

Hi, I'm Caramon "Fox" Stanley.

I got into event and esports photography because I love capturing the energy and emotion of competitive gaming.

Background

I started shooting esports events in 2023 for TSS at HCS Charlotte and have since covered other major events like the Halo Championship Series, Comic-Con, and DreamHack. My work focuses on capturing the intensity of competition, the excitement of the crowd, the stories behind the players and fans, and the incredible cosplayers showing their craftsmanship and skill.
